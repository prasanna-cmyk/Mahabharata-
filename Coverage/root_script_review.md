# Bounded factual and continuity review: working groups 23–39

Reviewed 2026-09-06. Read the two raw episode JSON files, selected assembled scenes in `outputs/Mahabharata/Project Files/Data/Master_Scenes.json`, and the supplied Volume 3/4 TXT bodies. This is a selective factual review, not a verse completeness certification. Raw scene IDs below map to assembled `Gxx-Snn` IDs; the assembler has already resolved the raw E24S08 collision. ## 1. E35S07 / G35-S07 changes the Viprithu encounter into discovery and consent

**Consequential correction.** Narration says Viprithu consents after learning who the travelers are and their relationship. The image instruction explicitly says `no invented battle`. The source actually contains a nonlethal armed encounter and says Viprithu already knew the situation and had been appointed by Krishna to intercept/speak to the couple.

Source: `MAHABHARATA-4-AADIPARVA_djvu.txt`, 12173–12281: Arjuna promises to fight without wounding Subhadra's relatives; Subhadra drives into the force; arrows are exchanged; Arjuna cuts weapons and chariot equipment; Viprithu stops his troops. Lines 12295–12313: he embraces Arjuna and says nothing of the events is unknown to him. Lines 12348–12350: Krishna appointed him to speak with the couple on the route.

Correction: narrate the controlled encounter before the embrace, prior knowledge, blessing and onward journey. Replace the image prohibition with a source-specific nonlethal action shot or split encounter and blessing into two scenes. Avoid a new lethal battle.

## 2. E25S05–06 / G25-S05–06 hides who ignites the lacquer house

**Consequential causal omission.** Spoken text says only that fire breaks out and the Pandavas escape. Since the preceding story establishes Purochana's arson plan, this can make the audience infer that he launches the attack. In this source the Pandavas choose the timing and Bhima ignites the house after the others enter the tunnel.

Source: `MAHABHARATA-3-AADIPARVA_djvu.txt`, 9489–9502: Yudhishthira proposes escape by burning the house and Purochana. Lines 9581–9595: Bhima sends Kunti and his brothers into the tunnel, gives fire to the house, then escapes. The six deaths are consequential, not an anonymous accident.

Correction: state Bhima's agency and the prior decision directly once, without a technical arson procedure. Also this edition identifies the Nishada woman as Kunti's established friend (9544–9545, 9563–9566), whereas the scene presents an unidentified arriving woman. Retain that relationship while avoiding the source's moral epithet as an objective narrator judgment.

## 3. E35S06 / G35-S06 loses Subhadra's actual role as charioteer

**Consequential action omission.** Both riders are merely seated/alert in the prompt. This is an important physical expression of her participation in this edition, especially because the package emphasizes her agency in narration.

Source: `MAHABHARATA-4-AADIPARVA_djvu.txt`, 11841–11850: Subhadra states her skill with reins and mounts first to take them. Lines 11879–11884 again identify her holding the reins. Lines 12205–12206 show her driving into Viprithu's force at Arjuna's direction.

Correction: make Subhadra visibly hold the reins and control the team through the departure and encounter, with Arjuna handling his bow. The two-horse count is supported later by named Shaibya and Sugriva at 12424–12425; no reason to alter that count.

## 4. E29S06 / G29-S06 substitutes a general moral for the ancestors' decisive revelation

**Consequential explanation gap.** The present text says the ancestors stop Aurva because wrath must not destroy innocent worlds. That sentiment is compatible with part of their speech, but omits the revelation that changes Aurva's understanding of the massacre: they say they deliberately provoked their own deaths, rather than having been unable to defend themselves. Without it, the causal resolution has been replaced by a generic moral.

Source: `MAHABHARATA-3-AADIPARVA_djvu.txt`, 23275–23300: the Bhrigu ancestors deny helplessness, explain weariness with prolonged life, and say the buried wealth was intended to provoke the Kshatriyas. Lines 23314–23327 explain their stated reason for seeking death through another's act; 23335–23338 urge Aurva to stop world destruction.

Correction: add the ancestors' specific account as attributed mythic speech, not the narrator's endorsement of arranging death or of collective violence. Then retain Aurva's vow problem and the ocean-fire resolution. This is a source-specific reversal, more important than decorative expansion.

## 5. E39S07 and E39S06 have source ranges that miss key narrated events

**Evidence correction.** E39S07's current range 19126–19265 cannot support the Indra boon: it precedes that scene. Bounds validation alone cannot detect this.

Correct E39S07 to `MAHABHARATA-4-AADIPARVA_djvu.txt` 19274–19335: Indra descends, offers boons, conditions the weapons on Shiva's favor, and grants Krishna lasting affection with Arjuna. Extend E39S06's father/reunion source to at least 19074–19252, which includes the conversation and departure (current end 19125 cuts the account short).
